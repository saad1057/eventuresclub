A meadow is not a venue until it has beds, a road that your guests can stand, and a weather plan that is not a prayer. Eventures Club will plan a nikah in the hills. We will not describe it as easier than a hall in Lahore because the photograph has fewer walls. The opposite is usually true. The hall has power, kitchens and a gate. The hill has a view, and then a list of problems that are all solvable if the guest list is small and the date is honest.

The size is the first cut. A nikah for about twelve people, with a night on either side, is a pleasure in Nathiagali or in a quiet corner of Swat. People arrive, sleep, dress, and leave without turning the ceremony into a convoy. A barat of a hundred, transplanted to Karimabad for a same-night return to Lahore, is not a pleasure. It is a logistics problem wearing a landscape. If that is the wedding you want, keep the wedding in Lahore and take a smaller group north on another week. The [weddings](/events/weddings) page says this in the same words we will say on the phone.

Lahore remains the main work. Mehndi, barat and walima in the city or in a farmhouse on its edge are planned as a sequence of rooms, meals and relatives. The useful questions are which gate the cars use, when the last table is served, and who holds the timeline when a photographer wants ten more minutes. Flowers are part of that work. They are not a substitute for it. We do not publish fake case studies of rooms we did not build. The pictures on the event pages are atmosphere. If you want to see a real night, ask, and we will talk about one we can actually stand behind.

For a hill nikah the season matters as much as it does for a tour. Nathiagali can hold a small ceremony from spring into autumn, and a winter date is only romantic if every guest has agreed to cold. Swat in July may be wet. Hunza in October is beautiful and already full of travellers, so rooms for even twelve people need an early brief, not a month's notice. The drive from Lahore to Hunza is two days. Guests who are also coming from Karachi need that fact in the invitation, not as a surprise at the airport in Islamabad. A ceremony that ignores the road will be attended by the people who live closest, and resented by everyone else.

What we need in the [event brief](/plan/event) is plain. The type of event, so we know it is a wedding and not a dinner. A city, or the sentence "help us find one". A date or a month. A guest count that includes the cousins, because a hidden twenty people becomes a food problem at eight in the evening. Indoor or outdoor, or a request that we decide. Whether catering is ours to arrange. A budget band, so we do not design a ceiling of flowers for a nikah that wanted a quiet hour and a good table. The notes field is for the grandmother who should not climb, the family that wants the nikah silent and the dinner loud, and the colour everyone is tired of.

Travel for the guests is a separate muscle, and it is one this club actually has. A northern nikah often wants a vehicle plan, rooms and a pace that belongs to the tour desk as well as the event desk. We still will not make you fill one tangled form. The event brief carries the gathering. If the journey needs its own plan, we open that beside it and you do not retype your name. Prices for tours on this site are not the price of a wedding. A [Hunza tour](/tours/hunza-valley) starts at PKR 85,000 a person for a travelling group of four. A ceremony has rooms for people who are not on a twin-share holiday, plus the hour itself. Those are different invoices.

After you send the form, the email you receive is your own answers, and the desk receives the same. The next note from us is a direction: where, what is possible on that date, and what we are refusing to promise. Nothing is held until you say the direction is right. Payment, if the plan goes ahead, is on an invoice. The website does not take it.

If the date is impossible we will say so. A July Friday departure from Lahore for a Saturday morning nikah in Hunza is impossible. A Friday departure for a Sunday hour in Nathiagali can work if the guests leave early and the ridge is not shut with holiday traffic. The difference is the whole job. Write the true date. We would rather change the valley than discover the truth in a traffic jam above Murree.
